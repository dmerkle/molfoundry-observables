# ROP and FRAGGER

The retained comparisons use the current ROP population and sequence
projections. Their shared inputs, normalization and limits are documented
in [the comparison guide](../scripts/comparison/README.md).

The population comparison supplies a singleton-gate invariant to FRAGGER,
not a target basis or equations. Equality is checked against the current
composition-derived molecular generator. It is not molecular graph-input
identity or a cross-tool performance comparison.
